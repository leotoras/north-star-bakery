Our Story

North Star Bakery started in a small home kitchen. Our founder baked bread for friends and neighbors, and soon everyone wanted more. Today we have a shop on Maple Street, but we still bake the same way: by hand, with care, and for the people who live around us.

The North Star helps people find their way home. We want our bakery to feel like home too.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We keep our list short and simple.

  • We buy flour, eggs, and milk from local farms when we can.
  • We use real butter and no fake flavors.
  • We bake in small batches so nothing sits around.
  • We give leftover bread to a local food pantry.
