What We Bake

Everything here is made by hand in our kitchen. Prices are a range because they change with size and flavor. You do not pay online. Just send us a pre-order request and pay when you pick up.

Signature Loaf

Breads

Sourdough, country loaves, baguettes, and sandwich bread. Great for family dinners and lunches.

Price range: $6 to $10 per loaf

Pastries

Buttery croissants, sweet rolls, muffins, and cookies. Perfect for a quick breakfast on your commute.

Price range: $3 to $6 each. Boxes of 6 or 12 are $18 to $30.

Cakes

Birthday cakes, small celebration cakes, and cupcakes. We can write a message on top. Event planners, please ask us about large orders.

Price range: $30 to $90 for cakes, $24 to $40 per dozen cupcakes
